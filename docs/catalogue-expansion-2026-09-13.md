## Facts that change a learner’s decision

- Claude exams require Claude Partner Network membership access. Public Claude Academy learning is a separate option. The catalogue does not claim public exam access or that a course certificate equals a proctored credential. [Anthropic programme announcement](https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications), [partner route](https://claude.com/partners), [public academy](https://academy.claude.com/).
- AIGP belongs to IAPP. Its AI governance focus differs from privacy management, privacy technology and jurisdiction-specific CIPP credentials. [AIGP](https://iapp.org/certify/aigp), [CIPM](https://iapp.org/certify/cipm), [CIPT](https://iapp.org/certify/cipt), [CIPP](https://iapp.org/certify/cipp).
- Salesforce’s current catalogue uses Platform Administrator II, Platform Developer, Agentforce Sales/Service Consultant and Data 360 Consultant. Familiar former names remain searchable. [Administrator catalogue](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/credentials/administratoroverview), [developer catalogue](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/en/credentials/developeroverview), [consultant catalogue](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/en/credentials/consultantoverview).
- Cisco’s current Automation and Cybersecurity titles are used. CCNP is a multi-exam credential; CCIE also requires a practical lab. [Cisco catalogue](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/training-certifications/certifications/index.html). Each added track links its individual official page.
- ISACA’s advanced AI credentials have qualifying-designation requirements. AAIA, AAISM and AAIR also state testing-centre-only delivery for residents of India, Mainland China and Hong Kong. These restrictions appear in the affected records. [AAIA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaia), [AAISM](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaism), [AAIR](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aair).
- PMI requirements depend on the credential and application route. Pages direct learners to credential-specific education, training and experience criteria, including special learning routes. [PMI catalogue](https://www.pmi.org/certifications), [PMI-CPMAI](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/ai-project-management-cpmai), [PMI-PMOCP](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/pmo-certified-professional-pmi-pmocp).
- Databricks distinguishes proctored certification exams from course badges and accreditations. The existing eight exam tracks match its current catalogue. [Catalogue](https://www.databricks.com/learn/training/certification), [registration FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq).

## Registration sources and limits

Registration instructions link directly to [Salesforce/Pearson](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/salesforce.html), [Cisco/Pearson](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/cisco.html), [CompTIA/Pearson](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/comptia.html), [IAPP’s process](https://iapp.org/certify/certification-process), and the selected provider’s own credential pages. The instructions are an overview; they do not replace the provider’s application or book an exam for a visitor.

CompTIA’s main catalogue and store could not be retrieved during this review. Credential scope was checked through CompTIA’s issuer-owned Credly pages, linked individually in the data. Pearson confirms the CompTIA Central registration route. Current exam codes and retirement dates were not independently verified, so no new CompTIA exam-series codes, prices or appointment guarantees are published. CompTIA records explicitly say to confirm the live version before purchase. DataAI/DataX naming and other unverified additions were left out.

## Editorial and trust choices

Role fit, career relevance and suggested projects are editorial guidance. No hiring, salary or exam-pass guarantee is added. The homepage’s three career journeys are explicitly labelled illustrative examples at section and card level. They do not use invented customer names, photographs, star ratings, quotes or achieved outcomes. Replace them with customer testimonials only after receiving authentic feedback and permission to publish it.

The homepage exposes every provider, adds a dedicated Claude section and expands the showcase and WhatsApp enquiry choices. Registration instructions appear on every credential page and in provider-specific expandable guides. Claude’s guide is open by default so restricted access is visible before selection.
